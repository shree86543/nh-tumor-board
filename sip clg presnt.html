03. Core Diagnostic Tool: Root Cause Analysis

The '5 Whys' Breakdown

To optimize a system, we must diagnose the baseline problem down to its true structural failure point. Surface fixes produce short-term solutions; drilling down exposes the foundational constraint.

Foundational Discovery:

Leakage in high-margin oncology procedures is not caused by clinical deficits. It stems from sequential routing delays across unlinked specialist offices, creating a high-stress conversion environment.

1 Losing high-acuity cases to competitors.
2 Patients leave after initial outpatient consultations.
3 They become anxious waiting for treatment path execution.
4 The multidisciplinary care plan takes too long to solidify.
5 Clinical files are manually routed across separate offices.
Root Cause: Absence of a unified digital intake layer to instantly trigger concurrent consensus.

04. Theoretical Pillars: Goldratt & Lean Optimization

Theory of Constraints (TOC)

Under Eliyahu Goldratt’s TOC framework, systemic velocity is strictly limited by the single slowest constraint within the architecture.

"In outpatient conversion and ward requests, the system constraint was the human intermediary. Processing tickets sequentially through a manual helpdesk operator capped total network capacity."
Action: Elevate constraint via programmatic routing.

Lean Waste (Muda) Elimination

Lean service logic demands the systematic tracking and removal of non-value-adding waste to protect margins.

"Legacy dispatch loops relied on repeated verbal phone steps. This architecture targeted processing and motion waste, replacing loose data handoffs with single-touch transaction captures."
Action: Standardize inputs to eliminate data decay.

05. Behavioral Reasoning: Choice Architecture

Choice Architecture & Friction Cost

Behavioral economics dictates that user compliance is directly shaped by cognitive load and friction. If an action breaks intuitive, daily behavioral paths, users drop out.

Central Indian Demographic Reality:

The active patient base includes older, non-digitally native profiles. Forcing navigation through specialized app stores and password registrations creates severe "App Fatigue," rendering legacy portals highly ineffective.
