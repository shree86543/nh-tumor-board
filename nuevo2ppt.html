Operational Breakdown
Demographic Friction vs. Low Native App Adoption
The "App Fatigue" Barrier

Our current app requires steps that alienate our primary patient profiles:

  • UX Roadblocks: Play Store navigation, password setups, and profile registration create heavy friction.
  • Demographic Realities: The majority base consists of older and less tech-literate patients who struggle with standalone app environments.
  • Asset Underutilization: High installation drop-off renders the current system ineffective for large-scale transaction management.
The Downstream Cost Leaks

Because digital adoption is stalled, traditional bottlenecks persist:

  • Unpredictable Walk-ins: Leads to highly volatile morning surges and prolonged patient turnaround times (TAT).
  • High Manual Tracking Costs: Floor staff lose hours manually addressing routine queries ("Are lab reports ready?", "What is my bill?").
  • Consultation Vacancy: High appointment drop-offs go unrecovered without quick, accessible re-engagement loops.

Backend Architecture & Flow Management
Preventing Traffic Collision on a Single Number

The Question: "If we use the same WhatsApp number for both the IPBOT and the Outpatient Assistant, won't there be confusion in the backend process flow?"

Primary Solution (Tier 2)
Parameterized Entry Points

Patients don't just type "Hi". We provide specific QR codes that contain "pre-filled" hidden text commands.

  • In the Inpatient Wards: The QR code taped to the bed table generates a hidden message like IP_START. The backend instantly launches the inpatient food/services menu.
  • At the OPD Registration Desk: The QR code on the standee generates a hidden message like OP_START. The backend launches the outpatient booking menu.
Manual Fallback (Tier 3)
The Universal Triage Menu

If someone manually types the number without scanning a QR code (e.g., a relative messaging from home), the bot acts as a digital receptionist:

"Welcome to Narayana Health Raipur! To assist you better, please choose an option:

Reply 1: I am currently admitted in the hospital (Inpatient Services)
Reply 2: I want to book an appointment or check lab results (Outpatient Services)"
